What it is

SAM is a browser-based, old-school arena shooter built as a homage to the 2001 classic: huge sun-bleached Egyptian arenas, circle-strafing at 10.5 m/s, screaming headless kamikazes, charging bulls, a ten-gun arsenal and a skyscraper-sized boss. Every texture, model, sound effect and music track is generated procedurally at load time, so the repo ships no binary assets and runs offline. The only dependency is a vendored copy of Three.js (MIT).

Name note: this is an unofficial fan homage. It uses original procedural art/audio and descriptive enemy names rather than any of the original game's assets or trademarks.

Run it locally

ES modules need to be served over HTTP (opening index.html via file:// will not work). Pick one:

# Node (no install needed)
npx http-server -c-1 -p 8080 .

# Python
python -m http.server 8080

# Docker (Windows PowerShell, from the repo folder)
docker build -t sam . ; docker run --rm -p 8080:80 sam

Then open http://localhost:8080 in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. A WebGL2-capable GPU is required; any discrete GPU (for example a Legion laptop's RTX) runs the High/Ultra presets comfortably.

Deploy

It is a static site: GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages or any S3 bucket works as-is. There's no build step.

Bestiary

HP is scaled by difficulty (Tourist ×0.6 … Serious ×1.5); damage dealt to you is scaled ×0.35 … ×1.8.

Headless Kamikaze

10 HP9.5 m/s

Sprints at you screaming with a bomb in each hand and detonates on contact (4.2 m blast). Killing one near its friends starts a chain reaction. Backpedal and shoot.

Headless Rocketeer

25 HPranged

Keeps 9–22 m away, strafes, and lobs led fireballs when it has line of sight. Pillars block its shots.

One-Eyed Gnasher

40 HPmelee

A furry ball of teeth. Charges and bites for 10. Hops up ledges if you camp on a platform.

Horned Skeleton

90 HPleaper

Lopes at 9 m/s, leaps across gaps and throws twin chain-balls. Headshots deal double damage.

Horned Bull

220 HP16 m/s charge

Picks a point 12 m past you and charges with limited turning. Sidestep late; getting hit deals 28 plus heavy knockback.

Arachnid Soldier

500 HP14-round bursts

Scorpion-bodied gunner. Plants itself at ~26 m and hoses you with twin guns, and its tail stings anything close. Use rockets.

Winged Harpy

40 HPflying

Circles overhead, spits venom, then dives for a claw strike and climbs away. Track it with the tommygun.

Biomechanoid

700 HPhoming rockets

Heavy walker firing paired homing rockets every ~3 s. Break line of sight behind pillars and let the rockets hit stone.

Ra, the Sun Colossus (final boss)

22 000 HPsun nova

A golden rematch crowned by a blazing sun disc. On top of the Colossus attacks it fires expanding rings of fireballs. Strafe through the gaps.

The Colossus (boss)

14 000 HP22 m tall

Cycles fireball volleys, cannon barrages, a ground-stomp shockwave (jump to dodge it: it only hurts grounded targets) and summons kamikazes, harpies and gnashers. Enrages below 50% HP.

Levels

1 · Temple of the Sun

day100 enemies

Hatshepsut-style courtyard, a corridor, then a temple plaza with sphinxes. Finds: shotgun, tommygun, double coach gun, a super-health in a corner.

2 · Oasis of Scorpions

dusk116 enemies

A palm-ringed oasis, then a walled fortress with a stepped central platform. Finds: rocket launcher, minigun, Serious Damage.

3 · Valley of the Moon

night133 enemies

Torch-lit basalt halls under a starfield, then a valley lined with jackal-headed statues. Finds: grenade launcher, laser gun.

4 · The Great Pyramid

blood-red skyboss

A warm-up arena, then a 140×140 m amphitheatre before the Great Pyramid for the Colossus. Destroying it opens the portal onward. Finds: SBC Cannon and stockpiles of ammo.

5 · Sandstorm Siege

sandstormsurvival · 199 enemies

Hold a 110 m desert fortress for 2 min 30 s while nine timed assault waves pour in through blowing sand. A countdown replaces the objective; when it hits zero, finish the stragglers. Towers, a central keep and sandbag cover.

6 · Halls of the Duat

underworldlava · jump pads

Rivers of molten lava burn anything that wades in. Launch pads throw you across the flames and onto the Hall of Judgement's moated dais. Embers drift through the red dark.

7 · Sky Temple of Ra

floating islandsfinal boss

Temple islands above a sea of clouds, linked by railless bridges and launch pads. Falling off is fatal. At the summit waits Ra, the Sun Colossus, who adds a radial sun-nova attack to the Colossus moveset.

Continue on the main menu resumes from the last level you finished, keeping your arsenal. Weapons, ammo, bombs, health and armor carry between levels. Clearing an arena saves a checkpoint; dying offers a respawn there. Choosing a later level from Select Level grants a fair starting loadout.

Technical notes

Rendering
  • Three.js r186 WebGL renderer, HalfFloat MSAA×4 target → UnrealBloom → OutputPass (ACES filmic tone mapping, sRGB).
  • First-person weapons render in a second scene/camera pass with a depth clear, so guns never clip into walls.
  • Shader sky with sun disc, FBM clouds, stars and moon, plus a PMREM environment map baked from that sky per level for metal reflections.
  • One directional shadow map follows the player, texel-snapped to avoid shimmering. Pooled point lights (fixed count, so shaders never recompile) are used for muzzle flashes and explosions.
  • Level walls use world-space UVs merged into one mesh per material; static decor (pillars, statues, obelisks…) is merged at load, taking an empty arena to ~125 visible objects.
  • Particles: two GPU point pools (additive fire/sparks, alpha blood/smoke) with swap-remove simulation, plus a pooled gib, decal and tracer system.
Procedural content
  • Textures: tileable value-noise FBM painted into canvases with per-pixel height, converted to normal maps (sandstone blocks, carved hieroglyph friezes, cracked tiles, rippled sand, basalt, bark, palm fronds).
  • Models: every enemy is a rig of primitives with named pivots animated procedurally (gait cycles, wing flaps, tail sway, recoil).
  • Audio: 48 effects are synthesised once through OfflineAudioContext (formant-filtered kamikaze scream, distorted roars, gunshots, explosions), then played with distance attenuation and stereo panning and per-group voice limits.
  • Music: a look-ahead Web Audio sequencer in the Hijaz / Phrygian-dominant scale that crossfades between ambient pads and a 148 BPM drum/bass/power-chord combat layer based on how many enemies are alive.
Physics & AI
  • The world is a set of axis-aligned boxes in a 6 m spatial hash. Supported queries: cylinder push-out, ground height with step-up, ceiling checks and a DDA grid-walking ray cast.
  • Player movement is Quake-style (friction + wish-direction acceleration, sub-stepped collision at high speed).
  • Enemies steer toward/around the player, separate from each other, sidestep when blocked, hop onto ledges, lead their shots and respect line of sight.
  • Encounters spawn staggered waves in game time, clamp enemies to their arena bounds (so none can be lost outside), lock/unlock doors and save checkpoints.

Testing

npm install          # dev deps: three, playwright
npm test             # runs all three suites below
node tests/unit.mjs          # collision / ray math (12 tests)
node tests/smoke.mjs         # boots in headless Chromium, 20+ scenarios incl. all new mechanics, screenshots to test-results/
node tests/playthrough.mjs   # a bot plays all 7 levels to completion + final victory (LEVEL=n for one level)

The playthrough bot aims, switches weapons and fires through the real weapon/projectile code, relocating when it loses line of sight. It asserts every encounter triggers, every wave spawns, every door opens and every exit portal (or the boss victory) is reachable. It already caught a mid-iteration crash when the boss died and a case where melee enemies couldn't reach a player standing on a platform.
